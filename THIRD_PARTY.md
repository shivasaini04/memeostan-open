## Artwork

On 2026-10-31, Shiva Saini confirmed that the original project artwork was
created by him using AI. It is included on that stated provenance. No claim is
made to third-party characters or trademarks that may appear in generated art.
AI generation by itself does not establish rights to underlying characters.

The most recognizable character stickers (Nyan Cat, Creeper, Trollface, the frog
politician and Doge politician) have been replaced with original AI-generated
mascots for this public edition. Replacement provenance is listed below.

## Audio

`Claws_vs_Teeth.mp3` was excluded from this public copy because its recording
and composition provenance have not been confirmed. No app source references it.

## Fonts

The CSS requests Anton, Permanent Marker, Caveat, Outfit, Press Start 2P and
Bungee from Google Fonts. Browsers contact Google for these fonts; they are not
bundled in this repository. Fonts retain their respective licenses.

## Public-edition replacements (2026-10-31)
